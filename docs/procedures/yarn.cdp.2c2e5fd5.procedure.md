1. Click **New Draft** to open a new draft in the editor.
2. Enter a title in the **Untitled** title field; the verified example used **Scratch Cursor Type Demo 1337700534**.
3. Open **Project actions**, then select **Screen Clip Settings...**.
4. In this draft’s **Screen Clip Settings**—a per-draft setting—open the **Cursor Style** dropdown.
5. Select the desired cursor type; the verified example used **Pointer-first** instead of **Arrow-first**.
6. Click **Done** to commit the change and close the panel.
7. To confirm persistence, reopen **Project actions** → **Screen Clip Settings...**. Verify that **Cursor Style** shows the selected value, such as **Pointer-first**, and no longer shows the former value.
