1. Click **New Draft** to open an **Untitled** scratch draft.
2. In the current draft’s **Screen Recording Settings**, set **Cursor Style** to the desired option; the verified choice was **Pointer-first**. This is a per-draft screen clip setting, not a brand-wide default.
3. Click **Done** to save the change and close the settings panel.
4. Open **Project actions**, then click **Screen Clip Settings...**
5. Confirm **Screen Recording Settings** reopens for the **Untitled** draft and **Cursor Style** shows **Pointer-first** rather than **Arrow-first**.
